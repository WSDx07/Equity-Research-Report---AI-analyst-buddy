Dollar Tree, Inc. Reports Third Quarter Results
•Total revenue increased 6.9% to $5.1 billion
•Comparable store net sales increased 4.6% on top of 4.2% last year
•Comparable store net sales growth included a 3.8% increase in traffic and a 0.8% increase in average ticket
•Diluted EPS of $1.24, including $0.42 of cost related to the net impact of tariff reinvestments1
•Adjusted EPS of $1.16, including $0.42 of cost related to the net impact of tariff reinvestments
•Operating income margin contracted 110 basis points including a 195 basis point cost related to the net impact of tariff reinvestments

•Returned $205 million to shareholders through share repurchases in Q3
•Increasing fiscal 2026 adjusted EPS outlook to a range of $8.05 to $8.25 including an approximate $0.65 benefit related to the net impact of tariff refunds
•Q4 fiscal 2026 outlook of 3% to 4% comparable store net sales growth and adjusted EPS of $2.46 to $2.66 including an approximate $0.25 cost related to tariff refund reinvestments
CHESAPEAKE, Va. – November 24, 2026 – Dollar Tree, Inc. (NASDAQ: DLTR) today reported financial results for its third quarter ended October 31, 2026.
“Our third quarter results show the strength of Dollar Tree’s value proposition and the progress we’ve made to improve the customer experience,” said Mike Creedon, Chief Executive Officer. “Comp traffic accelerated to 3.8% and drove comparable sales above the high end of our outlook. Our broader assortment, better stores and stronger customer engagement give shoppers more reasons to choose Dollar Tree and come back more often. We continue to invest in our brand, our stores and our people to build on this momentum. As the holiday season begins, our focus is clear: make Dollar Tree the destination for value, convenience and discovery, and position the business for sustained, profitable growth.”

Third Quarter Results
Results for the third quarter ended October 31, 2026 are presented on a continuing operations basis. Continuing operations reflect the results of the Dollar Tree U.S. and Dollar Tree Canada banners.
Unless otherwise noted, all comparisons are to the prior-year third quarter ended November 1, 2025 for the results of continuing operations.
Net sales increased 6.9% to $5.1 billion. Comparable store net sales increased 4.6%, driven by a 3.8% increase in traffic and a 0.8% increase in average ticket.
Gross profit margin decreased 10 basis points to 35.7% and included 45 basis points of net cost from tariff refund reinvestment. Absent these factors, gross margin was primarily driven by lower tariff costs, lower markdowns and favorable shrink trends, partially offset by higher product and freight costs and unfavorable sales mix.
Selling, general and administrative expenses increased 70 basis points to 29.9% of total revenue. SG&A included 150 basis points of tariff refund reinvestment, of which $40 million was attributable to a charitable donation. Absent these factors, SG&A was primarily driven by lower payroll.
Adjusted selling, general and administrative expenses inclusive of transition services agreement income net, increased 110 basis points as a percent of total revenue and included 150 basis points attributable to tariff refund reinvestment.
Transition services agreement income, net was $10 million for services provided between Dollar Tree and Family Dollar following the sale, compared with $24 million in the prior-year period.
2
Operating income was $309 million and operating income margin was 6.1%. Operating income margin decreased 110 basis points versus the prior-year period and included 195 basis points of net cost from tariff refund reinvestments. Adjusted operating income margin decreased 120 basis points, including 195 basis points of net tariff reinvestment cost.
The Company’s effective tax rate was 24.5%, compared with 23.9% in the prior-year period.
Income from continuing operations was $232 million and diluted earnings per share was $1.24, including a $0.42 cost related to the net impact of tariff reinvestments. On an adjusted basis, income from continuing operations was $218 million and adjusted diluted earnings per share was $1.16.
The Company repurchased 1.8 million shares of its common stock during the third quarter of fiscal 2026 for $205 million, excluding applicable excise tax.
As of October 31, 2026, the Company had $2.3 billion remaining under its share repurchase authorization, $966 million of cash and cash equivalents, no commercial paper outstanding, and no borrowings under its revolving credit facility.
Year-to-Date Results
Results for the 39 weeks ended October 31, 2026 are presented on a continuing operations basis. Continuing operations reflect the results of the Dollar Tree U.S. and Dollar Tree Canada banners.
Unless otherwise noted, all comparisons are to the prior-year 39 weeks ended November 1, 2025 for the results of continuing operations.
Net sales increased 7.0% to $14.9 billion. Comparable store net sales increased 4.0%, reflecting a 2.9% increase in average ticket and a 1.1% increase in traffic.
Gross profit increased 16.5% to $5.7 billion and gross profit margin expanded 310 basis points to 38.4%, including a 210 basis point benefit from the net impact of tariff refunds and related reinvestments. Absent these factors, gross margin was primarily impacted by favorable shrink, lower tariff costs, and lower markdowns, partially offset by unfavorable sales mix and higher freight costs.
Selling, general and administrative expenses were 28.9% of total revenue, including a 60 basis point cost from tariff reinvestments, compared with 28.7% in the prior-year period. Absent these factors SG&A was primarily driven by lower payroll expenses partially offset by higher marketing, general liability, and depreciation expense.
Adjusted selling, general and administrative expenses inclusive of transition services agreement income net, increased 20 basis points as a percent of total revenue.
Transition services agreement income, net was $49 million for services provided between Dollar Tree and Family Dollar following the sale.
3
Operating income increased 54% to $1.47 billion and operating income margin expanded 290 basis points to 9.8%. Adjusted operating income margin expanded 290 basis points, including a 145 basis point benefit related to the net impact of tariff refunds and related reinvestments.
The Company’s effective tax rate was 24.9%, compared with 25.1% in the prior-year period. Income from continuing operations was $1.1 billion and diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was $5.70, including a $0.90 net benefit related to tariff refunds and related reinvestments.
The Company repurchased 12.9 million shares for $1.4 billion, excluding applicable excise tax.
Fiscal 2026 Outlook
•Net sales from continuing operations of $20.6 billion to $20.7 billion, based on comparable store net sales growth of 3.5% to 4.0%.
•Approximately 400 new store openings and 75 closings.
•Adjusted diluted EPS of $8.05 to $8.25, including approximately $0.65 related to the net impact of tariff refunds and tariff refund reinvestments.
Fourth Quarter 2026 Outlook
•Net sales from continuing operations of $5.7 billion to $5.8 billion, based on comparable store net sales growth in the range of 3% to 4%.
•Fourth quarter adjusted diluted EPS is expected to be in the range of $2.46 to $2.66, including an approximate $0.25 cost impact related to tariff refund reinvestments.

Free cash flow may not represent the amount of cash flow available for general discretionary use, because it excludes non-discretionary expenditures, such as mandatory debt repayments and required settlements of recorded and/or contingent liabilities not reflected in cash flow from operations. Historically, the company has excluded these items from non-GAAP financial measures. The company currently expects to continue to exclude these items in future disclosures of non-GAAP financial measures and may also exclude other items that may arise (collectively, “non-GAAP adjustments”).
