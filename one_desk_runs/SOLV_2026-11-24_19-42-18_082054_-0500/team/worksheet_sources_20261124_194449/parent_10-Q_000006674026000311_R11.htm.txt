000007 | Note 5. Restructuring Actions
000008 | Transformation Costs: In the third quarter of 2025, 3M began a transformation program intended as a structural redesign of longer-term manufacturing, distribution, and business process services and locations. In the third quarter and first nine months of 2026, management approved and committed to additional actions resulting in a pre-tax charge of $27 million and $145 million, respectively, primarily employee related. Charges related to this initiative are reflected in Corporate (see Note 16) and primarily impacted selling, general and administrative expenses and cost of sales. The accrued restructuring liability was $25 million as of December 31, 2025 and $53 million as of September 30, 2026, reflecting new charges partially offset by cash payments. Additional actions are expected and are subject to management's future approval and commitment.
