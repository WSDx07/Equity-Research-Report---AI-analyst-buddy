- Data Center was **92.91882556131260794473229706%** of Q3 FY2027 revenue ($107.6B / $115.8B). This is a concentration observation, not a forecast. [NVIDIA earnings release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27pr.htm)
- The 10-K says NVIDIA invested over **$76.7 billion** in research and development since inception. This is cumulative, not FY2026 R&D expense. [NVIDIA FY2026 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/0001045810-26-000021-index.htm)
- FY2026 operating margin is **60.38%**, calculated as $130.387B operating income / $215.938B revenue x 100. Both are FY2026 figures. [NVIDIA FY2026 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/0001045810-26-000021-index.htm)
- The 10-K supports expansion in the U.S. and Latin America; the earlier added supplier-redundancy rationale is removed. Export-control exposure is described as a company-disclosed risk, not an independently proven market-share outcome. [NVIDIA FY2026 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/0001045810-26-000021-index.htm)
- Suggestions to diversify manufacturing or develop a China-compliant product are AI suggestions, not NVIDIA commitments, and are removed from the company-fact section.
- Debt wording is corrected: noncurrent debt was $7.469B vs. $8.463B in FY2025; current long-term debt was $0.999B vs. zero. Combined debt was $8.468B vs. $8.463B, up $5M. These figures do not establish that NVIDIA relied mainly on internal financing. [NVIDIA FY2026 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/0001045810-26-000021-index.htm)
