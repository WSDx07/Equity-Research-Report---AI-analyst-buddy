## Investment Recommendation

**Buy** — GE Vernova is positioned as a global leader in power generation, grid solutions and electrification, with a sizable backlog, accelerating order growth and expanding margins. The company’s strategic acquisition of a high‑voltage grid specialist promises to broaden its product portfolio and capture scale benefits, while disciplined cost‑improvement initiatives and a strong cash‑generation track record underpin its financial resilience. However, the business faces execution risks around integration, quality and supply‑chain dependencies, and intense competition from other major equipment manufacturers.
