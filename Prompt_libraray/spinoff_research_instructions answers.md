  ### Part 1: Reading, Factors, and Window Length

  #### 1. Sentence About the Reading

  │ Institutional investors frequently sell shares of a newly distributed spinoff immediately because the new entity is

  │ too small for their portfolio minimums, falls outside their fund's investment mandate, does not pay an approved

  │ dividend, or is excluded from index benchmarks that their fund must replicate.

  #### 2. Selected Factors List

  • Factor 1: Company size relative to parent

      • Direction: Lower market capitalization relative to the former parent gets more points.

      • Weight: 25 points

      • Reading: Joel Greenblatt, You Can Be a Stock Market Genius, Chapter 3.

      • Rationale: A spinoff that represents a small fraction of the parent company often causes parent shareholders to

      sell the distributed shares as an unwanted fractional holding.

  • Factor 2: Early post-distribution price weakness

      • Direction: Larger percentage price decline from the first regular-way close gets more points.

      • Weight: 25 points

      • Reading: Joel Greenblatt, You Can Be a Stock Market Genius, Chapter 3; Seth Klarman, Margin of Safety, Chapter

      10.

      • Rationale: Indiscriminate initial selling creates short-term downward price pressure before market participants

      evaluate standalone business fundamentals.

  • Factor 3: Insider ownership and management incentives

      • Direction: Higher percentage of insider equity ownership gets more points.

      • Weight: 25 points

      • Reading: Joel Greenblatt, You Can Be a Stock Market Genius, Chapter 3.

      • Rationale: Direct executive share ownership aligns management incentives with standalone equity appreciation.

  • Factor 4: Wall Street analyst coverage

      • Direction: Fewer sell-side research analysts covering the company get more points.

      • Weight: 25 points

      • Reading: Joel Greenblatt, You Can Be a Stock Market Genius, Chapter 3; Seth Klarman, Margin of Safety, Chapter

      10.

      • Rationale: Lack of institutional and analyst coverage allows valuation disparities to persist longer.

  #### 3. Window Choice

  • Window: 90 days after the distribution date.

      • Rationale: Greenblatt and Klarman report that initial institutional selling and price discovery typically run

      their course within 1 to 3 months (30 to 90 days) following the distribution.

  ### Part 3: Form 10 Filer That Is Not a Spinoff

    NOT A SPINOFF

    Company or fund name: KKR FS Income Trust Select (CIK: 0001975736; Unlisted statutory trust)

    Form type: Form 10-12G

    Filing date: February 2, 2024

    Why I think it is not a spinoff:

    The registrant is a newly formed Delaware statutory trust that filed Form 10 under Section 12(g) of the Securities
