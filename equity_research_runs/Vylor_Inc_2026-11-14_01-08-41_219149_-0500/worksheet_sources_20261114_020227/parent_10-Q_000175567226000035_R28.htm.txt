000008 | RELATED PARTY TRANSACTIONS
000009 | Transactions with Corteva
000010 | EIDP and Corteva, including certain consolidated subsidiaries (collectively the “Participating Companies”), are party to a physical cash pooling arrangement which facilitates the management of the cash and liquidity needs of the Participating Companies.
000011 | For the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, EIDP declared cash dividends to Corteva, Inc. amounting to $118 million and $745 million, respectively. EIDP paid dividends of $129 million and $870 million respectively, to Corteva, Inc. during the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026. For the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, EIDP declared cash dividends to Corteva, Inc. amounting to $732 million and $1,620 million, respectively, of which $372 million and $1,260 million, respectively, were paid to Corteva, Inc. during the periods.
000012 | As of September 30, 2026, December 31, 2025 and September 30, 2025, EIDP had dividends payable to Corteva, Inc. of $8 million, $133 million and $360 million, respectively.
000013 | As of September 30, 2026, December 31, 2025 and September 30, 2025, EIDP had payables to Corteva, Inc. of $22 million, $27 million and $17 million included in accrued and other current liabilities, respectively, and $105 million, $154 million and $145 million included in other noncurrent obligations, respectively, in the interim Consolidated Balance Sheets related to Corteva’s indemnification liabilities to Dow and DuPont per the Corteva Separation Agreements (refer to the section entitled “Guarantees” of Note 12 - Commitments and Contingent Liabilities, to the interim Corteva Consolidated Financial Statements, for further details of the Corteva Separation Agreements).
