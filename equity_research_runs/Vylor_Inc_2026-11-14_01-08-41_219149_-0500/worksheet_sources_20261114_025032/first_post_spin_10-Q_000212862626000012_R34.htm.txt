000008 | Contract Balances
000009 | Contract liabilities primarily reflect deferred revenue from prepayments under contracts with customers where the Company receives advance payments for products to be delivered in future periods. The Seed Business classifies deferred revenue as current or noncurrent based on the timing of when the company expects to recognize revenue. Contract assets primarily include amounts related to conditional rights to consideration for completed performance not yet invoiced. Accounts receivable are recorded when the right to consideration becomes unconditional.
