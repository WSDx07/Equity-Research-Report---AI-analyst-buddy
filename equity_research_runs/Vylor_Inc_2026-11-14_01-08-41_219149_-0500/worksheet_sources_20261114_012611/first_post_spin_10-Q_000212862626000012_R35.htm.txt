000007 | RESTRUCTURING AND ASSET RELATED CHARGES - NET
000008 | Corteva periodically assesses its business priorities and operational structure to maximize the customer experience and deliver on growth and earnings potential. The Seed Business records charges attributable to it in association with these committed restructuring actions. The restructuring actions associated with these programs are expected to be substantially complete by December 2026. At September 30, 2026 and 2025, the liability associated with these programs was $15 million and $5 million, respectively. The remaining cash payments related to these charges, which primarily consist of severance and related benefits payments, are expected to be paid over the remainder of the year.
