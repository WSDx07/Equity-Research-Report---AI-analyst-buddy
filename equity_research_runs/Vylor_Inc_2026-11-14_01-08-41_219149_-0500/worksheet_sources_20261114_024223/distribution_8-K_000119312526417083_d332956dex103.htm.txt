000007 | PURPOSE
000008 | This Change in
000009 | Control and Executive Severance Plan has been established by the Company on October 1, 2026 (the “Effective Date”) to provide certain employees of the Company with the opportunity to receive certain severance protections. The
000010 | Plan, as set forth herein, is primarily intended to help retain qualified employees, maintain a stable work environment and provide economic security to eligible employees in the event of certain qualifying terminations of employment. Capitalized
000011 | terms used but not otherwise defined herein have the meanings set forth in Article II.
000012 | The Plan is not intended to be included in the
000013 | definitions of “employee pension benefit plan” or “pension plan” set forth under Section 3(2) of ERISA. The Plan is intended to meet the descriptive requirements of a plan constituting a “severance pay plan”
000014 | within the meaning of regulations published by the Secretary of Labor at Title 29, Code of Federal Regulations, Section 2510.3-2(b). Notwithstanding the foregoing, if and to the extent that the Plan is
000015 | deemed to be an “employee pension benefit plan” or “pension plan” as set forth under Section 3(2) of ERISA, then the Plan is intended, for all purposes under ERISA, to constitute a plan that is unfunded and maintained by
000016 | the Company primarily for the purposes of providing deferred compensation for a select group of management or highly compensated employees.
