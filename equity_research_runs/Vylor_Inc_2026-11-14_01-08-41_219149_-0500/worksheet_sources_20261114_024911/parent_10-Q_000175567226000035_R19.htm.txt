000036 | Debt Offerings
000037 | In September 2026, Corteva issued $700 million of 6.00 percent Senior Notes due in August 2036 (“Corteva 2036 Notes”). The proceeds of this offering are intended to be used for general corporate purposes, including the repayment of commercial paper borrowings.
000038 | In August 2026, Vylor issued $1.1 billion aggregate principal amount of senior notes in a private offering, consisting of $550 million aggregate principal amount of Senior Notes due 2031 (“Vylor 2031 Notes”) and $550 million aggregate principal amount of Senior Notes due 2036 (“Vylor 2036 Notes”). The Vylor 2031 Notes bear interest at a rate of 5.125 percent per year and mature in August 2031. The Vylor 2036 Notes bear interest at a rate of 5.625 percent per year and mature in August 2036.
000039 | In May 2025, Corteva issued $500 million of 5.125 percent Senior Notes due in May 2032 (“Corteva 2032 Notes”). The proceeds were used to repay the $500 million senior notes that matured in July 2025.
000040 | August 2026 Exchange Offers and Consent Solicitations
000041 | In August 2026, Vylor commenced private offers to exchange any and all of the outstanding $500 million principal amount of 2.30 percent Senior Notes due 2030 (the “EIDP 2030 Notes”), the $500 million principal amount of 5.125 percent Senior Notes due 2032 (the “EIDP 2032 Notes”), and the $600 million principal amount of 4.80 percent Senior Notes due 2033 (the “EIDP 2033 Notes”), in each case, issued by EIDP, Inc., (collectively, the “EIDP Notes”), to the extent held by eligible holders (“Eligible Noteholders”), for a corresponding series of notes to be newly issued by Vylor (collectively, the “Vylor Notes”), having the same interest payment dates, maturity date and interest rate as the respective EIDP Notes (with respect to each series, an “Exchange Offer" and together, the “Exchange Offers”). Concurrently with the Exchange Offers, with respect to each series of EIDP Notes, Vylor solicited the consents of Eligible Noteholders, on behalf of EIDP, to amend the indentures governing the EIDP Notes (together, the “Consent Solicitations”) to eliminate substantially all of the restrictive covenants and events of default (other than payment and bankruptcy related events of default) therefrom.
000042 | On October 1, 2026, Vylor settled the Exchange Offers and obtained the corresponding Consent Solicitations. Under the Exchange Offers, $1,439 million aggregate principal amount of EIDP Notes, comprising $435 million aggregate principal amount of the EIDP 2030 Notes, $476 million aggregate principal amount of the EIDP 2032 Notes and $528 million aggregate principal amount of the EIDP 2033 Notes were exchanged and are no longer debt obligations of Corteva.
000043 | The Exchange Offers and Consent Solicitations were made upon the terms and conditions set forth in an exchange offer memorandum and consent solicitation statement, dated August 6, 2026 (the “Offering Memorandum”). The Vylor Notes are subject to covenants and events of default that are typical for companies with similar credit ratings, and which are described in the Offering Memorandum.

000055 | Corteva Credit Facilities
000056 | Revolving Credit Facilities
000057 | In May 2022, the company entered into a $3 billion, five-year revolving credit facility and a $2 billion, three-year revolving credit facility (the “Revolving Credit Facilities”) expiring in May 2027 and May 2025, respectively. In June 2024, the Revolving Credit Facilities were refinanced for purposes of extending the maturity dates for the five-year and three-year revolving credit facilities to June 2029 and June 2027, respectively, and lowering the facility amount of the five-year revolving credit facility to $2.85 billion and the three-year revolving credit facility to $1.9 billion. In August 2026, the five-year revolving credit facility was refinanced at $2.85 billion for purposes of extending the maturity date to August 2031 and decreasing the facility amount to $2.0 billion upon consummation of the Vylor Separation on October 1, 2026. In addition, the three-year revolving credit facility was terminated upon consummation of the Vylor Separation. Borrowings under the Revolving Credit Facilities will have an interest rate equal to Term SOFR plus the applicable margin. The Revolving Credit Facilities may serve as a substitute to the company’s commercial paper program, and can be used, from time to time, for general corporate purposes including, but not limited to, the funding of seasonal working capital needs. The Revolving Credit Facilities contain customary representations and warranties, affirmative and negative covenants and events of default that are typical for companies with similar credit ratings. Additionally, the Revolving Credit Facilities contain a financial covenant requiring that the ratio of total
000058 | indebtedness to total capitalization for Corteva and its consolidated subsidiaries not exceed 0.60. At September 30, 2026, the company was in compliance with these covenants.
000059 | In February 2026, the company amended its January 2023 (as amended in July 2023, January 2024, February 2024 and February 2025) 364-day revolving credit agreement (the “364-Day Revolving Credit Facility”), increasing the facility amount from $750 million to $1.25 billion, extending the expiration date to February 2027 and amending the interest rate to Term SOFR plus the applicable margin. In February 2025, the company amended the 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility, decreasing the facility amount from $1 billion to $750 million and extending the expiration date to February 2026. In May 2026, the company drew down $600 million under the 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility, to repay the $600 million senior notes that matured in May 2026. On October 1, 2026, borrowings outstanding under the 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility were repaid, and the facility was terminated. The 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility included a provision under which Corteva may convert any advances outstanding prior to the maturity date into term loans having a maturity date up to one year later. The 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility contained customary representations and warranties, affirmative and negative covenants and events of default that are typical for companies with similar credit ratings. Additionally, the 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility contained a financial covenant requiring that the ratio of total indebtedness to total capitalization for Corteva and its consolidated subsidiaries not exceed 0.60. At September 30, 2026, the company was in compliance with these covenants.
000060 | Delayed Draw Term Facility
000061 | In August 2026, the Company entered into a $1 billion delayed draw term facility (the “Delayed Draw Term Facility”). The maturity date of the Delayed Draw Term Facility is approximately one year from its closing date. Borrowings under the Delayed Draw Term Facility have an interest rate equal to Term SOFR plus the applicable margin. The Delayed Draw Term Facility was intended to serve as a backstop to the Corteva 2036 Senior Notes. The commitments under the Delayed Draw Term Facility were automatically and permanently reduced, on a dollar-for-dollar basis, by an amount equal to the aggregate principal amount of the Corteva 2036 Senior Notes issued in the September 2026 Debt Offering. The balance of the capacity was voluntarily and permanently reduced on September 30, 2026. The Delayed Draw Term Facility was subject to mandatory prepayment (and, prior to funding, automatic and permanent commitment reduction) requirements from the net cash proceeds of debt and equity issuances by Corteva. Corteva was permitted to voluntarily prepay delayed draw term loans, and to voluntarily reduce undrawn commitments, without a penalty. The Delayed Draw Term Loan Credit Agreement contained covenants and events of default substantially similar in scope and terms to those described above for the Revolving Credit Facilities.
000062 | Vylor Credit Facilities
000063 | In August 2026, Vylor entered into (i) a five-year senior unsecured revolving credit facility in an aggregate principal amount equal to $3.0 billion (the “Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility”), (ii) a 364-day senior unsecured revolving credit facility in an aggregate principal amount equal to $1.5 billion (the “Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility” and, together with the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility, the “Vylor Revolving Credit Facilities”), and (iii) a senior unsecured delayed draw term loan facility in an original principal amount equal to $2.75 billion (the “Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility” and, together with the Vylor Revolving Credit Facilities, the “Vylor Credit Facilities”).
000064 | Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility
000065 | Vylor, along with its subsidiary, Pioneer Hi-Bred International, Inc. (“PHI”), are initially co-borrowers under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility. Prior to the Five-Year RCF PHI Release Date (as defined below), each of Vylor and PHI is entitled to request loans under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility, in each case subject to the terms and conditions thereof, and each of Vylor and PHI guarantees, on a joint and several basis, obligations of the other co-borrower under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility. The Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility became effective on October 1, 2026.
000066 | Upon the earlier of (i) December 31, 2026 and (ii) the first date on which all obligations of PHI under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility are paid in full in cash (such date, the “Five-Year RCF PHI Release Date”), PHI will automatically cease to be a borrower under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility and will be automatically released as a guarantor thereunder. From and after the Five-Year RCF PHI Release Date, Vylor will be the sole borrower under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility, and immediately thereafter no subsidiaries of Vylor will guarantee such facility.
000067 | Proceeds of the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility are otherwise intended to be used for general corporate purposes of Vylor and its subsidiaries. The Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility is also intended to serve as a backstop to its commercial paper program. The maturity date of the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility is five years from its closing date. Amounts borrowed under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility are subject to an interest rate per annum equal to
000068 | Term SOFR plus the applicable margin. On October 1, 2026, Vylor borrowed $1,578 million under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility to repay borrowings outstanding under the PHI Bilateral Facility (as defined below).
000069 | The Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Agreement contains customary representations and warranties, affirmative and negative covenants and events of default that are typical for companies with similar credit ratings. Additionally, the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Agreement contains a financial covenant requiring that the ratio of total indebtedness to total capitalization for Vylor and its consolidated subsidiaries not exceed 0.60.
000070 | Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility
000071 | Vylor, along with its subsidiary, PHI, are initially co-borrowers under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility. Prior to the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility PHI Release Date (as defined below), each of Vylor and PHI is entitled to request loans under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility, in each case subject to the terms and conditions thereof, and each of Vylor and PHI guarantees, on a joint and several basis, obligations of the other co-borrower under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility. The Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility became effective on October 1, 2026.
000072 | Upon the earlier of (i) December 31, 2026 and (ii) the first date on which all obligations of PHI under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility are paid in full in cash (such date, the “364-Day RCF PHI Release Date”), PHI will automatically cease to be a borrower under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility and will be automatically released as a guarantor thereunder. From and after the 364-Day RCF PHI Release Date, Vylor will be the sole borrower under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility, and immediately thereafter no subsidiaries of Vylor will guarantee such facility.
000073 | Proceeds of the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility are intended to be used for general corporate purposes of Vylor and its subsidiaries. The Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility is also intended to serve as a backstop to its commercial paper program. The maturity date of the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility is 364 days from its closing date. Amounts borrowed under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility are subject to an interest rate per annum equal to Term SOFR plus the applicable margin. On October 1, 2026, Vylor borrowed $1,500 million under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility to repay borrowings outstanding under the PHI Bilateral Facility (as defined below).
000074 | The Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Agreement includes a provision under which Vylor may convert any advances outstanding prior to the maturity date into term loans having a maturity date up to one year later. 000075 | Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility
000076 | The Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility, with a capacity of up to $2.75 billion, was intended to serve as a backstop to the Exchange Offers and the issuance of the Vylor 2031 Notes and the Vylor 2036 Notes. The commitments under the Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility were automatically and permanently reduced, on a dollar-for-dollar basis, by an amount equal to the aggregate principal amount of Vylor Notes issued in the Exchange Offers and by the aggregate principal amount of the Vylor 2031 Notes and the Vylor 2036 Notes. Vylor was the sole borrower under the Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility.
000077 | Proceeds of loans under the Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility were intended to be used to finance the Vylor Separation and to pay fees, costs and expenses related thereto. The maturity date of the Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility was approximately one year from its closing date. Amounts borrowed under the Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility were subject to an interest rate per annum equal to Term SOFR plus the applicable margin. There were no borrowings outstanding under the Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility, and the facility was terminated on October 1, 2026.
000078 | The Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility was subject to mandatory prepayment (and, prior to funding, automatic and permanent commitment reduction) requirements from the net cash proceeds of debt and equity issuances by Vylor. Vylor was permitted to voluntarily prepay delayed draw term loans, and to voluntarily reduce undrawn commitments, without a penalty. The Vylor Delayed Draw Term Loan Credit Agreement contained covenants and events of default substantially similar in scope and terms to those described above for the Vylor Revolving Credit Facilities.
000079 | PHI Bilateral Facility
000080 | PHI entered into a loan facility up to an aggregate principal amount of $3.5 billion with Banco Bilbao Vizcaya Argentaria, S.A. New York Branch in August 2026 (the “PHI Bilateral Facility”), which was used for the repayment of a portion of the outstanding borrowings under EIDP’s commercial paper program. The PHI Bilateral Facility had commitments from September 1, 2026 through September 5, 2026, after which commitments were automatically terminated. The PHI Bilateral Facility was
000081 | expected to serve as a bridge to the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility and Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility, each of which were drawn upon consummation of the Vylor Separation to repay borrowings outstanding on the PHI Bilateral Facility. Following this repayment, on October 1, 2026, the PHI Bilateral Facility ceased to be effective.
000082 | PHI is the sole borrower under the PHI Bilateral Facility. Amounts borrowed under the PHI Bilateral Facility are subject to an interest rate per annum equal to Term SOFR plus the applicable margin. The PHI Bilateral Credit Agreement contains covenants and events of default substantially similar in scope and terms to those described above for the Vylor Revolving Credit Facilities.
