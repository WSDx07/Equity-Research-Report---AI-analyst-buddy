000025 | Debt Offerings
000026 | In August 2026, Vylor issued $1.1 billion aggregate principal amount of senior notes in a private offering, consisting of $550 million aggregate principal amount of Senior Notes due 2031 (“Vylor 2031 Notes”) and $550 million aggregate principal amount of Senior Notes due 2036 (“Vylor 2036 Notes”). The Vylor 2031 Notes bear interest at a rate of 5.125 percent per year and mature in August 2031. The Vylor 2036 Notes bear interest at a rate of 5.625 percent per year and mature in August 2036.
000027 | August 2026 Exchange Offers and Consent Solicitations
000028 | In August 2026, Vylor commenced private offers to exchange any and all of the outstanding $500 million principal amount of 2.30 percent Senior Notes due 2030 (the “EIDP 2030 Notes”), the $500 million principal amount of 5.125 percent Senior Notes due 2032 (the “EIDP 2032 Notes”), and the $600 million principal amount of 4.80 percent Senior Notes due 2033 (the “EIDP 2033 Notes”), in each case, issued by EIDP, Inc., (collectively, the “EIDP Notes”), to the extent held by eligible holders (“Eligible Noteholders”), for a corresponding series of notes to be newly issued by Vylor (collectively, the “Vylor Notes”), having the same interest payment dates, maturity date and interest rate as the respective EIDP Notes (with respect to each series, an “Exchange Offer" and together, the "Exchange Offers”). Concurrently with the Exchange Offers, with respect to each series of EIDP Notes, Vylor solicited the consents of Eligible Noteholders, on behalf of EIDP, to amend the indentures governing the EIDP Notes (together, the “Consent Solicitations”) to eliminate substantially all of the restrictive covenants and events of default (other than payment and bankruptcy related events of default) therefrom.
000029 | On October 1, 2026, Vylor settled the Exchange Offers and obtained the corresponding Consent Solicitations. Under the Exchange Offers, $1,439 million aggregate principal amount of EIDP Notes, comprising $435 million aggregate principal amount of the EIDP 2030 Notes, $476 million aggregate principal amount of the EIDP 2032 Notes and $528 million aggregate principal amount of the EIDP 2033 Notes were exchanged and are no longer debt obligations of Corteva.
000030 | The Exchange Offers and Consent Solicitations were made upon the terms and conditions set forth in an exchange offer memorandum and consent solicitation statement, dated August 6, 2026 (the “Offering Memorandum”). The Vylor Notes are subject to covenants and events of default that are typical for companies with similar credit ratings, and which are described in the Offering Memorandum.

Interest rates are variable and determined at the time of borrowing. Total unused bank credit lines on the short-term and long-term foreign currency loans at September 30, 2026 was approximately $7 million. The company’s short-term foreign currency loans have varying maturities through 2026.

000041 | Vylor Credit Facilities
000042 | In August 2026, Vylor entered into (i) a five-year senior unsecured revolving credit facility in an aggregate principal amount equal to $3.0 billion (the “Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility”), (ii) a 364-day senior unsecured revolving credit facility in an aggregate principal amount equal to $1.5 billion (the “Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility” and, together with the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility, the “Vylor Revolving Credit Facilities”), and (iii) a senior unsecured delayed draw term loan facility in an original principal amount equal to $2.75 billion (the “Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility” and, together with the Vylor Revolving Credit Facilities, the “Vylor Credit Facilities”).
000043 | Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility
000044 | Vylor, along with its subsidiary, Pioneer Hi-Bred International, Inc. (“PHI”), are initially co-borrowers under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility. Prior to the Five-Year RCF PHI Release Date (as defined below), each of Vylor and PHI is entitled to request loans under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility, in each case subject to the terms and conditions thereof, and each of Vylor and PHI guarantees, on a joint and several basis, obligations of the other co-borrower under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility. The Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility became effective on October 1, 2026.
000045 | Upon the earlier of (i) December 31, 2026 and (ii) the first date on which all obligations of PHI under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility are paid in full in cash (such date, the “Five-Year RCF PHI Release Date”), PHI will automatically cease to be a borrower under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility and will be automatically released as a guarantor thereunder. From and after the Five-Year RCF PHI Release Date, Vylor will be the sole borrower under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility, and immediately thereafter no subsidiaries of Vylor will guarantee such facility.
000046 | Proceeds of the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility are otherwise intended to be used for general corporate purposes of Vylor and its subsidiaries. The Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility is also intended to serve as a backstop to its commercial paper program. The maturity date of the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility is five years from its closing date. Amounts borrowed under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility are subject to an interest rate per annum equal to Term SOFR plus the applicable margin. On October 1, 2026, Vylor borrowed $1,578 million under the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility to repay borrowings outstanding under the PHI Bilateral Facility (as defined below).
000047 | The Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Agreement contains customary representations and warranties, affirmative and negative covenants and events of default that are typical for companies with similar credit ratings. Additionally, the Vylor Five-Year Revolving
000048 | Credit Agreement contains a financial covenant requiring that the ratio of total indebtedness to total capitalization for Vylor and its consolidated subsidiaries not exceed 0.60.
000049 | Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility
000050 | Vylor, along with its subsidiary, PHI, are initially co-borrowers under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility. Prior to the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility PHI Release Date (as defined below), each of Vylor and PHI is entitled to request loans under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility, in each case subject to the terms and conditions thereof, and each of Vylor and PHI guarantees, on a joint and several basis, obligations of the other co-borrower under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility. The Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility became effective on October 1, 2026.
000051 | Upon the earlier of (i) December 31, 2026 and (ii) the first date on which all obligations of PHI under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility are paid in full in cash (such date, the “364-Day RCF PHI Release Date”), PHI will automatically cease to be a borrower under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility and will be automatically released as a guarantor thereunder. From and after the 364-Day RCF PHI Release Date, Vylor will be the sole borrower under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility, and immediately thereafter no subsidiaries of Vylor will guarantee such facility.
000052 | Proceeds of the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility are intended to be used for general corporate purposes of Vylor and its subsidiaries. The Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility is also intended to serve as a backstop to its commercial paper program. The maturity date of the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility is 364 days from its closing date. Amounts borrowed under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility are subject to an interest rate per annum equal to Term SOFR plus the applicable margin. On October 1, 2026, Vylor borrowed $1,500 million under the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility to repay borrowings outstanding under the PHI Bilateral Facility (as defined below).
000053 | The Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Agreement includes a provision under which Vylor may convert any advances outstanding prior to the maturity date into term loans having a maturity date up to one year later. The Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Agreement contains customary representations and warranties, affirmative and negative covenants and events of default that are typical for companies with similar credit ratings. Additionally, the Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Agreement contains a financial covenant requiring that the ratio of total indebtedness to total capitalization for Vylor and its consolidated subsidiaries not exceed 0.60.
000054 | Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility
000055 | The Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility, with a capacity of up to $2.75 billion, was intended to serve as a backstop to the Exchange Offers and the issuance of the Vylor 2031 Notes and the Vylor 2036 Notes. The commitments under the Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility were automatically and permanently reduced, on a dollar-for-dollar basis, by an amount equal to the aggregate principal amount of Vylor Notes issued in the Exchange Offers and by the aggregate principal amount of the Vylor 2031 Notes and the Vylor 2036 Notes. Vylor was the sole borrower under the Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility.
000056 | Proceeds of loans under the Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility were intended to be used to finance the Vylor Separation and to pay fees, costs and expenses related thereto. The maturity date of the Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility was approximately one year from its closing date. Amounts borrowed under the Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility were subject to an interest rate per annum equal to Term SOFR plus the applicable margin. There were no borrowings outstanding under the Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility, and the facility was terminated on October 1, 2026.
000057 | The Vylor Delayed Draw Term Facility was subject to mandatory prepayment (and, prior to funding, automatic and permanent commitment reduction) requirements from the net cash proceeds of debt and equity issuances by Vylor. Vylor was permitted to voluntarily prepay delayed draw term loans, and to voluntarily reduce undrawn commitments, without a penalty. The Vylor Delayed Draw Term Loan Credit Agreement contained covenants and events of default substantially similar in scope and terms to those described above for the Revolving Credit Agreements.
000058 | PHI Bilateral Facility
000059 | PHI entered into a loan facility up to an aggregate principal amount of $3.5 billion with Banco Bilbao Vizcaya Argentaria, S.A. New York Branch in August 2026 (the “PHI Bilateral Facility”), which was used for the repayment of a portion of the outstanding borrowings under EIDP’s commercial paper program. The PHI Bilateral Facility had commitments from September 1, 2026 through September 5, 2026, after which commitments were automatically terminated. The PHI Bilateral Facility was expected to serve as a bridge to the Vylor Five-Year Revolving Credit Facility and Vylor 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility, each of which were drawn upon consummation of the Vylor Separation to repay borrowings outstanding on the PHI Bilateral Facility. Following this repayment, on October 1, 2026, the PHI Bilateral Facility ceased to be effective.
000060 | PHI is the sole borrower under the PHI Bilateral Facility. Amounts borrowed under the PHI Bilateral Facility are subject to an interest rate per annum equal to Term SOFR plus the applicable margin. The PHI Bilateral Credit Agreement contains covenants and events of default substantially similar in scope and terms to those described above for the Vylor Revolving Credit Facilities.
