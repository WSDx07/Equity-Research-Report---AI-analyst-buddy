000006 | I. Purpose; Effective Date and Term
000007 | (A)
000008 | Purpose. The purpose of the Vylor Inc. Global Omnibus Employee Stock Purchase Plan (the
000009 | “Plan”) is to afford Eligible Employees an opportunity to obtain a proprietary interest in the continued growth and prosperity of Vylor Inc. (the “Company”) through ownership of shares of the Company’s common stock
000010 | (“Common Stock”). The Company intends for the Plan to be comprised of two components: a component with offerings that are intended to qualify as an “employee stock purchase plan” under Code Section 423 (the “Code
000011 | Section 423 Component”), and a component with offerings that are not intended to qualify as an “employee stock purchase plan” under Code Section 423 (the “Non-Code
000012 | Section 423 Component”). The provisions of the Code Section 423 Component shall be construed so as to extend and limit participation on a uniform and nondiscriminatory basis consistent with the requirements of Code Section 423.
000013 | A right to purchase shares of Common Stock under the Non-Code Section 423 Component shall be effectuated via separate offerings under one or more sub-plans of the Non-Code Section 423 Component of the Plan for Employees of Participating Non-423 Subsidiaries and affiliates in countries outside of the United States in order to
000014 | achieve tax, employment, securities law or other purposes and objectives, and to conform the terms of the sub-plans with the laws and requirements of such countries. Except as otherwise provided herein or in
000015 | the applicable sub-plan, the Non-Code Section 423 Component of the Plan shall be operated and administered in the same manner as the Code Section 423
000016 | Component.
000017 | (B)
000018 | Effective Date and Term. The Plan shall become effective on January 1, 2027 and shall continue in
000019 | effect until the date on which all of the shares of Common Stock authorized for issuance under the Plan have been issued.
