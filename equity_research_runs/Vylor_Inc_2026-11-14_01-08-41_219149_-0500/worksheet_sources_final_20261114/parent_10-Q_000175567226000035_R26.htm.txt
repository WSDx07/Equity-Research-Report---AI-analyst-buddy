000007 | SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
000008 | The Vylor Separation and Distribution
000009 | On October 1, 2026, the Vylor Separation from Corteva, Inc. was completed.
000010 | In connection with the Vylor Separation, Corteva and Vylor entered into various agreements to effect the Vylor Separation and provide a framework for their relationship after the Vylor Separation, including the Separation and Distribution Agreement, Tax Matters Agreement, Employee Matters Agreement, Transition Services Agreements, Commercial Agreements, Intellectual Property Matters Agreement and other ancillary agreements. These agreements provide for the allocation between Corteva and Vylor of assets, employees, liabilities and obligations (including property and employee benefits and tax-related assets and liabilities) attributable to periods prior to, at, and after the Vylor Separation from Corteva and govern certain relationships between Corteva and Vylor after the Vylor Separation.
000011 | On October 1, 2026, in connection with the consummation of the Vylor Separation, Corteva transferred the Seed Business to Vylor, in exchange for, among other consideration, a cash distribution from Vylor of $761 million. Corteva used the proceeds to pay down the $600 million balance outstanding under the 364-Day Revolving Credit Facility and its outstanding commercial paper balance of $161 million. Refer to Note 11 - Short Term Borrowings, Long-Term Debt and Available Credit Facilities, to the interim Consolidated Financial Statements, for additional information regarding the debt transactions related to the Vylor Separation. Subsequent to the Vylor Separation, Vylor transferred $183 million to Corteva in settlement of an intercompany note that was issued by Vylor to Corteva as part of pre-separation arrangements associated with the establishment of the Vylor and Corteva starting capital structures. Additionally, Corteva transferred $82 million to Vylor subsequent to the Vylor Separation in finalizing the opening balance sheets of the companies.
