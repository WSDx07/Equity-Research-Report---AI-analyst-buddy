000007 | SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
000008 | No recognizable subsequent events have occurred after September 30, 2026, but before November 12, 2026, which is the date the interim Consolidated Financial Statements of Corteva for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, from which these interim Combined Financial Statements are derived, were issued.
000009 | The Vylor Separation and Distribution
000010 | On October 1, 2026, the Vylor Separation from Corteva, Inc. was completed.
000011 | In connection with the Vylor Separation, Corteva and Vylor entered into various agreements to effect the Vylor Separation and provide a framework for their relationship after the Vylor Separation, including the Separation and Distribution Agreement, Tax Matters Agreement, Employee Matters Agreement, Transition Services Agreements, Commercial Agreements, Intellectual Property Matters Agreement and other ancillary agreements. These agreements provide for the allocation between Corteva and Vylor of assets, employees, liabilities and obligations (including property and employee benefits and tax-related assets and liabilities) attributable to periods prior to, at, and after the Vylor Separation from Corteva and govern certain relationships between Corteva and Vylor after the Vylor Separation.
000012 | On October 1, 2026, in connection with the consummation of the Vylor Separation, Corteva transferred the Seed Business to Vylor, in exchange for, among other consideration, a cash distribution from Vylor of $761 million. Subsequent to the Vylor Separation, Vylor transferred $183 million to Corteva in settlement of an intercompany note that was issued by Vylor to Corteva as part of pre-separation arrangements associated with the establishment of Vylor and Corteva’s starting capital structures. Additionally, Corteva transferred $82 million to Vylor subsequent to the Vylor Separation in finalizing the opening balance sheets of the companies.
000013 | Dividend and Share Repurchase Program
000014 | In October 2026, Vylor’s Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.13 per share, payable on December 7, 2026 to stockholders of record as of November 30, 2026.
000015 | The Board also authorized a new $3 billion multi-year share repurchase program. Under the share repurchase program, which is immediately effective and does not expire, shares of Vylor’s common stock may be repurchased periodically in open-market or private transactions. The actual timing, number and value of shares repurchased under the Company's authorized share repurchase program will depend on a variety of factors determined by management, including the market price of Vylor common stock, general market and economic conditions, applicable legal requirements and other business considerations and uses of capital, including organic growth, dividends, and acquisitions.
