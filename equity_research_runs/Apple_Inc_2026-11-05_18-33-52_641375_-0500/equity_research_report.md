## Investment Recommendation

**Buy** — Apple leverages an integrated model that designs, manufactures, and markets hardware while expanding a portfolio of high‑margin services. The company’s diversified revenue base, rising services revenue at an all‑time high, and robust cash generation reinforce its growth trajectory. Strong profitability and a high return on equity demonstrate efficient capital deployment. Nonetheless, modest market share in core hardware categories, intense price competition, supply‑chain constraints for key components, reliance on limited sources, and ongoing regulatory and litigation exposures present material risks that temper the upside.
