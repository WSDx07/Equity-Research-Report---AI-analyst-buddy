000007 | Note 13. Earnings Per Share
000008 | On October 29, 2018, the date of consummation of the Spin-Off, 122,966,558 shares of the Company’s Common Stock, par value $0.001 per share, were distributed to Honeywell shareholders of record as of the October 16, 2018 Record Date. This share amount is being utilized for the calculation of basic earnings per share for all periods presented as no common stock was outstanding prior to the date of the Spin-Off. On October 29, 2018, the Company issued 1,601,037 time-based restricted stock units in connection with the Spin-Off. These restricted stock units were not included in the computation of diluted earnings per share for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2018.
