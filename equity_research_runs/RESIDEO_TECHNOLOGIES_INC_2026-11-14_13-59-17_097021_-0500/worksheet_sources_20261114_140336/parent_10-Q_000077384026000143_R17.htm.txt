000084 | Commercial Paper and Other Short-Term Borrowings
000085 | As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $2.4 billion of Commercial paper and other short-term borrowings outstanding at a weighted average interest rate of 3.85%. 000086 | Pre-Separation Funding
000087 | In March 2026, the Company entered into a series of debt transactions in anticipation of the Aerospace Spin-Off, in which Honeywell Aerospace issued an aggregate of $16.0 billion principal amount senior notes (the Aerospace Senior Notes). The Aerospace Senior Notes are senior unsecured obligations of Honeywell Aerospace, and were guaranteed on a senior unsecured basis by the Company until the Aerospace Spin-Off was completed. In connection with the Aerospace Spin-Off, the Aerospace Senior Notes were distributed to Honeywell Aerospace, and the Company was automatically and unconditionally released from all obligations under the guarantee.
000088 | Term Loan Agreements
000089 | In March 2026, the Company repaid its $1.0 billion 4.37% term loan due 2027. In August 2026, the Company made a partial prepayment of $1.5 billion on the $2.75 billion of borrowings outstanding under the 2025 term loan due 2027. As of September 30, 2026, there were $1.25 billion of borrowings outstanding under this term loan.
000090 | Revolving Credit Agreements
000091 | In March 2026, the Company entered into a $3.0 billion 364-day credit agreement (the 364-Day Credit Agreement). Upon completion of the Aerospace Spin-Off on June 29, 2026, the aggregate commitments thereunder were automatically reduced to $2.0 billion. The 364-Day Credit Agreement replaced the Company’s $3.0 billion 364-day credit agreement dated as of March 17, 2025, which was terminated in accordance with its terms effective March 6, 2026. Amounts borrowed under the 364-Day Credit Agreement are due no later than March 5, 2027, unless (i) the Company elects to convert all then outstanding amounts into a term loan, upon which such amounts shall be repaid in full on March 5, 2028, or (ii) the 364-Day Credit Agreement is terminated earlier pursuant to its terms.
000092 | In March 2026, the Company entered into a $4.0 billion five-year credit agreement (the Five-Year Credit Agreement). Upon completion of the Aerospace Spin-Off on June 29, 2026, the aggregate commitments thereunder were automatically reduced to $3.0 billion. The Five-Year Credit Agreement replaced the Company’s $4.0 billion five-year credit agreement dated as of March 18, 2024, which was terminated in accordance with its terms effective March 6, 2026. Commitments under the Five-Year Credit Agreement can be increased pursuant to the terms of the Five-Year Credit Agreement to an aggregate amount not to exceed $3.5 billion. Amounts borrowed under the Five-Year Credit Agreement are required to be repaid no later than March 6, 2031, unless such date is extended pursuant to the terms of the Five-Year Credit Agreement.
000093 | The 364-Day Credit Agreement and the Five-Year Credit Agreement are maintained for general corporate purposes.
000094 | As of September 30, 2026, there were no outstanding borrowings under the 364-Day Credit Agreement or the Five-Year Credit Agreement.
