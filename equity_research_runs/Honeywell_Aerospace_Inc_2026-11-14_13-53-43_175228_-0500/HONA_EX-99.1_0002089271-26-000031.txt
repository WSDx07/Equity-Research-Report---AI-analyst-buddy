Honeywell Aerospace reports third quarter results;
updates 2026 outlook
•Sales of $4.6 billion, reported and organic1 sales up 2% year over year
•Net income of $0.7 billion; adjusted EBIT1 of $1.2 billion, up 1% despite incremental standalone costs
•Raises full-year pro forma standalone adjusted earnings per share1 and second half free cash flow1 guidance
•Board declares inaugural quarterly dividend payment of $0.40 per share on the company’s common stock
PHOENIX, October 28, 2026 -- Honeywell Aerospace (Nasdaq: HONA) today announced results for the third quarter and declared its first quarterly dividend. The company also raised its full-year 2026 pro forma standalone adjusted earnings per share1 and second half 2026 free cash flow1 guidance ranges, maintained its full-year organic1 sales outlook, and narrowed its full-year pro forma standalone adjusted EBIT1 guidance range.
“Honeywell Aerospace delivered results above our expectations in its first quarter as a standalone company, increasing sales and adjusted EBIT sequentially and year over year as we accelerate investments to drive greater output and better serve our customers. Demand for our solutions remains robust with 9% orders growth over the last 12 months,” said Jim Currier, Chief Executive Officer of Honeywell Aerospace. “We are seeing encouraging signs from pacing suppliers as we deepen our commitment to enduring partnerships. This progress, combined with ongoing qualification of additional material sources, positions us for higher sales and output growth in 2027 and beyond.”
Initial dividend declaration
Honeywell Aerospace’s Board of Directors has approved a quarterly cash dividend of $0.40 per share of its common stock. The dividend is payable on December 4, 2026, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on November 13, 2026.
Currier added, “The Board’s decision to declare a regular dividend underscores the visibility into our durable free cash flow growth in the years ahead. Paying a competitive, growing dividend is a key component of our capital allocation strategy designed to deliver substantial shareholder value while maintaining financial flexibility and a strong investment-grade credit rating.”
Recent Honeywell Aerospace highlights
•Year to date, the company has secured more than $20 billion in new wins (estimated lifetime value) supported by the following announcements:
◦The U.S. Air Force advanced Honeywell Aerospace’s SkyShot 1600 engine, which is designed to meet all mission-critical performance parameters for collaborative combat aircraft and unmanned aircraft systems, into Preliminary Design Review as part of its Autonomous Collaborative Platform program.
◦Honeywell Aerospace secured over $2 billion in new awards to provide its advanced navigation and RF systems for several missiles and munitions programs, including some initial contracts on platforms prioritized by the Munitions Acceleration Council.
◦The City of Phoenix Aviation Department signed an agreement with Honeywell Aerospace to study how to translate the company’s Surface Alert (SURF-A) technology to ground vehicle operators on tarmacs, taxiways, and runways at three local airports, including Phoenix Sky Harbor International.

•The company announced a 300,000 square foot expansion of its Tempe, Arizona manufacturing facility, expected to open in late 2028. The new facility adds capacity, lean manufacturing, automation, and supply-chain resilience to support current Control Systems production, recent wins, and next generation platforms.
•Honeywell Aerospace presented an update on its supply chain transformation at a September investor conference. Key messages from the presentation included root causes and actions underway for the constraints from a small number of mechanical suppliers currently limiting output growth.
Summary financial results
Honeywell Aerospace sales for the third quarter increased 2% on a reported and organic¹ basis year over year. Backlog grew to $19.3 billion at quarter end, up 12% from the prior year, and trailing twelve-months orders were up 9%, led by Defense and Space. Adjusted EBIT1 was up 1%, including both an approximately $50 million net benefit from International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) tariff refunds and new costs necessary as an independent public company.

About Honeywell Aerospace
Honeywell Aerospace (Nasdaq: HONA) is an independent global aerospace and defense company whose critical technologies are broadly deployed on the world’s leading commercial air transport, business aviation, defense and space platforms. These integrated solutions enable safer, more efficient, and more reliable missions. Headquartered in Phoenix, Arizona, the company employs more than 36,000 people globally and supports more than 10,000 customers. With a broad portfolio spanning avionics and navigation systems, engines and power systems, and control systems for aircraft, Honeywell Aerospace combines commitment and deep engineering expertise to drive innovation and long-term value for the aerospace industry. For more information, visit www.honeywellaerospace.com or follow Honeywell Aerospace on LinkedIn.
