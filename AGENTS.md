## Equity research rules

For an equity research task, establish the company, ticker, and analysis date. If the user does not give an analysis date, use the current date and state it. For historical analysis, do not use information published after the analysis date.

  Give a traceable source and date for every material factual claim. For financial figures, state the reporting period, currency, and units. Distinguish reported facts, estimates, calculations, and judgments. Show the inputs and formula for each material calculation.

  Do not invent missing figures, sources, or quotes. Mark missing or conflicting evidence clearly. Preserve material conditions and uncertainty. Do not present a well-written report as evidence that its recommendation will outperform a benchmark.
