# Tyson Foods (TSN) — source-reviewed report

Analysis date: 2026-11-17.

## Business and customers

Tyson reported approximately 124,000 employees as of 2026-10-03. Its annual report describes a vertically integrated chicken operation and says it also processes cattle and hogs. The filing also describes Tyson New Ventures as an investment subsidiary for food-industry technologies, business models, and products. (2026 Form 10-K, Item 1, lines 130–135.) [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100493/000010049326000079/0000100493-26-000079-index.htm)

Walmart represented approximately 19.0% of FY2026 consolidated sales. No other single customer or customer group represented more than 10.0%. (2026 Form 10-K, “Customers,” line 169.)

## Results and outlook

The earnings release reports USD in millions, except per-share data. FY2026 sales were $56,419 million, versus $54,441 million in FY2025. GAAP operating income was $1,100 million, versus $1,098 million. Adjusted operating income was $1,977 million, versus $2,287 million. GAAP EPS was $1.28, versus $1.33; adjusted EPS was $3.44, versus $4.12. Adjusted measures are non-GAAP. (Form 8-K, Exhibit 99.1, lines 11–19.) [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100493/000010049326000077/0000100493-26-000077-index.htm)

Sales increased 3.6% year over year. The release says sales increased 1.1% after excluding the additional FY2026 week and legal-contingency accruals. FY2026 was a 53-week year; FY2025 and FY2024 were 52-week years. (Form 8-K, Exhibit 99.1, lines 19–21; Form 10-K, “Fiscal year,” line 454.)

For FY2027, management expects sales to decrease 3.5%–5.5% against FY2026 on a comparable 52-week basis, mainly due to lower Beef volumes from network restructuring and tight cattle supply. It expects capital expenditures of $1.1–$1.4 billion. (Form 8-K, Exhibit 99.1, “Outlook,” lines 112, 128, and 129–130.)

## Risks

Tyson says commodity and raw-material price increases may reduce margins when it cannot pass them through or offset them with productivity savings. The company also identifies price and promotion competition, animal-disease outbreaks, and climate effects on inputs, production, and supply chains as risks. (2026 Form 10-K, Item 1A, lines 221–226 and 290–292.)
