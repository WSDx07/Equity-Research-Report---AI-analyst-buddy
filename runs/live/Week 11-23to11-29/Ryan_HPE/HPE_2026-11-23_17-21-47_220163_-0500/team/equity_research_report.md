## Investment Recommendation

**Hold** — Hewlett Packard Enterprise is positioned at the intersection of networking, cloud and artificial intelligence, with a strategic portfolio and recent integration of Juniper Networks that gives it a full‑stack AI‑native networking offering. Revenue is accelerating thanks to strong demand for its Cloud & AI segment, but operating results have slipped into a breakeven position, reflecting margin pressure as the company shifts toward higher‑margin software and services and absorbs integration costs. The combination of robust top‑line momentum and current profitability challenges suggests a neutral stance until margins improve.
