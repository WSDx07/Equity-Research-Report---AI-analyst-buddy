# SemiLEDs Corp (LEDS)
Analysis date: 2026-11-27

## Overview

- The company develops, manufactures and sells LED chips, LED components, LED modules, sensor components and systems. — 10-K line 151: We develop, manufacture and sell light emitting diode (LED) chips and LED components, LED modules, sensor components incorporating LED or VCSEL and systems.
- Manufacturing starts with epitaxial growth of GaN layers on sapphire wafers followed by deposition of a reflective silver layer and copper alloy. — 10-K line 152: Utilizing our patented and proprietary technology, our manufacturing process begins by growing upon the surface of a sapphire wafer, or substrate, several very thin separate semiconductive crystalline layers of gallium nitride, or GaN, a process known as epitaxial growth, on top of which a mirror‑like reflective silver layer is then deposited.
- The firm optimizes epitaxial growth to improve light‑to‑electric conversion efficiency. — 10-K line 159: optimizing our epitaxial growth processes to create layers that efficiently convert electrical current into light;
- Technical capabilities allow production of LED chips, modules, sensor components and system products while reducing reliance on sapphire. — 10-K line 177: These technical capabilities enable us to produce LED chips, LED component, LED modules, sensor components incorporating LED or VCSEL and system products.
- The company operates a fabless model, using foundry fabs for chip ODM and seeking to sell idle chip‑making equipment. — 10-K line 201: We have moved to a fabless business model in which we utilize foundry fabs to ODM our chips using our developed technology and continue to explore opportunities to sell our chip manufacturing equipment, which will help us to reduce the idle capacity costs.
- Sales are conducted via a direct sales force, Japanese development partners and third‑party distributors. — 10-K line 210: We market and sell our products through both our direct sales force, Japanese development partners and distributors.
- Management plans to keep expanding sales coverage in Asia. — 10-K line 211: We plan to continue expanding our sales coverage in Asia as we grow our business.
- The firm provides technology‑development services leveraging its MOCVD EPI and advanced packaging expertise. — 10-K line 154: We provide technology services as a technology development partner for companies that value our MOCVD EPI and advanced packaging knowledge.
